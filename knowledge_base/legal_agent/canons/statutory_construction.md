# Canons of statutory construction

Interpretive rules for reading statutes. These are doctrine - long-settled
principles stated in countless opinions - not text from any copyrighted source.
They are rules about HOW to read, and they apply before any definition lookup.

## Order of operations

1. **Plain meaning.** If the text is unambiguous, it governs. Do not consult
   purpose, history or policy to create an ambiguity that the words do not have.
2. **Whole-text.** Read the provision in the context of the entire statute.
   A term is presumed to carry the same meaning throughout one act.
3. **Only then** the canons below, and only to resolve a genuine ambiguity.

## Language canons

- **Ejusdem generis** - where general words follow an enumeration of specific
  things, the general words are limited to things of the same kind. "Cars,
  trucks, motorcycles, and other vehicles" does not reach an aircraft.
- **Noscitur a sociis** - a word is known by the company it keeps; an ambiguous
  term takes colour from the terms surrounding it.
- **Expressio unius est exclusio alterius** - the expression of one thing
  implies the exclusion of others. An enumerated list of exceptions is presumed
  complete. Weak where the list reads as illustrative rather than exhaustive.
- **Rule of the last antecedent** - a qualifying phrase attaches to the
  immediately preceding antecedent, unless a comma separates them, in which case
  it may reach the whole series.
- **Surplusage canon** - prefer a reading that gives every word effect. An
  interpretation rendering a clause meaningless is disfavoured.
- **In pari materia** - statutes on the same subject are construed together.

## Substantive presumptions

- **Definitions section controls.** A term defined inside the instrument means
  what the instrument says it means, even where ordinary or dictionary usage
  differs. Always check for a definitions provision BEFORE consulting a
  dictionary.
- **Specific governs the general** where two provisions conflict.
- **Later-enacted governs earlier** on genuine conflict; repeal by implication
  is disfavoured.
- **Remedial statutes** are construed liberally toward their remedial purpose.
- **Statutes in derogation of common law** are construed narrowly.
- **Rule of lenity** - genuine ambiguity in a penal statute is resolved in
  favour of the defendant.
- **Constitutional avoidance** - where two readings are available, prefer the
  one that avoids a serious constitutional question.

## What this agent must NOT do with these

These are aids to reading, not authority to conclude. This agent performs
structural extraction. It records what an instrument says and how its terms
relate. It does not determine validity, enforceability, or outcome.
