# Body Interface v2 - prototype (read-only; nothing here touches the repo)

Open `index.html` in a browser (works from `file://`, no network). To see it talk to a mock ATI:
`python3 tools/mock_server.py 8765` then http://127.0.0.1:8765/ (answers `POST /execute` with the repo's generic ATI tables; **no natal record, no personal data**; `MOCK_HOUSES=1` adds clearly labelled SAMPLE planets).

## Third pass: feet (this pass)
* **Foot skin rebuilt twice** (feedback round 2 replaced the first third-pass toes, which were long, finger-like and separated by deep gaps). Body = Gray's Anatomy fig. 834 sole outline (public domain). Toes are redrawn (`tools/foot_skin.py`) as short, pressed-together capsules: hallux about 2.8 cm wide (base 3.1, tip 2.5), toe 2 about 1.5, toe 3 about 1.45, toe 4 about 1.4, toe 5 about 1.25 cm (hallux about 1.85x toe 2). Toe 2 tip is 0.25 cm short of the hallux tip, then the toe line curves back (tip y: -17.95, -17.7, -16.7, -15.6, -14.5; foot length 25.05 cm). Adjacent tip circles are nearly tangent, so the webs are only shallow, rounded notches (no cut slits), and the notch depth is recomputed from the outline. The forefoot (ball) is the widest part (9.6 cm, breadth/length 0.38), the outline narrows through the midfoot to a rounded heel, and a **medial arch concavity** (0.95 cm) is pulled into the outline; the bones were warped by the same function so they stay inside. Dorsal view: toenails (shorter, wider), one subtle crease per toe joint, short faint extensor-tendon streaks (metatarsal level only). Plantar view: ball, heel and toe pulp pads, plantar creases, a lighter non-weight-bearing medial arch zone and a darker lateral midfoot band. The toes are drawn straight in plan view; the slight flexion of real toes is not modelled.
* **True medial and lateral views** (`Foot medial`, `Foot lateral`): drawn profiles with the medial and lateral malleoli, tendon streaks, tibia/fibula stubs and bones. Medial: SP2-SP5, KI2-KI6, LR4 (+ EX-LE8). Lateral: BL60-BL66, GB40 and the lateral ankle points (+ EX-LE9). Points are in WHO depth order (BL60 above the lateral malleolus tip, BL62 below it, KI3 behind the medial malleolus, KI5 a full 1 B-cun = 2.1 cm below KI3, ...). Each `basis` states the assumption.
* **Whole-body front/back label density**: the ankle, foot and toe labels (and the foot extra points) only appear from zoom 6 (`FOOT_LABEL_Z` in `assets/app.js`; extras from zoom 8). The dots and the meridian lines are unchanged, and hovering or selecting a point still shows its label.
* **Dorsal/plantar crowding removed**: border and ankle points that are only readable in profile (`proj: true` in `points.json`) are hidden on the Foot top and Sole detail views and live on the medial/lateral views. On the **body (front/back) views they are still drawn**, so meridian lines stay continuous there; the feet on the body views are therefore still busy with labels (see below).
* **Foot scale on the body 7.62 px/cm** (was 6.73), equal to the body scale, with a smooth ankle stub that blends the ~6.7 cm foot into the ~6.2 cm AcuAtlas leg.
* **Licence**: the DBCLS bone drawings are dropped. Their Commons file pages still carry the `{{LicenseReview}}` marker, so the CC BY 4.0 licence is unreviewed there. The Bones layer on the foot is now schematic vector bones drawn from Gray plates 268/269/290/291 (public domain). Nothing from DBCLS ships.
* New views `foot_medial`, `foot_lateral` with jump buttons; Both/Right/Left/Fingertips/Wrist buttons work on them.
* Existing hooks, ids, views and house band 12 are unchanged (house 12 still reaches the toes; checked by `tools/test_feet.py`).

## Approximate / missing (be honest about this)
* **Every coordinate is an approximate placement** (`approx: true` + `basis` on each point). None is a measured or clinically verified location. Absolute sizes are assumptions: Gray's hand scaled to 18.7 cm, the foot to Gray fig. 834 scaled to about 25 cm skin length (heel to hallux tip, 25.05 cm), 1 B-cun = 2.1 cm, 0.1 f-cun = 0.2 cm.
* **Hand**: proportions and creases are Gray's (a drawn, idealised adult hand), but the soft-tissue shading, nails, lunulae, pads and tendon lines are drawn here, not traced. Nail size is a typical value. The skin is a stylised flat-shaded skin, not a render. Palmistry lines follow the creases but their routes, and the mounts (ellipses), are conventional drawings.
* **Forearm / leg joins**: LU7, PC5, HT4 etc. are hand-frame; the rest of the arm and leg points are AcuAtlas coordinates on its own figure (linear limbs, its own cun scale), so each wrist/ankle seam is only roughly right and the line steps where the frames meet. v1's re-ordering/outlier repairs on those body-frame limb points are kept (see each `basis`).
* **Foot scale on the body**: 7.62 px/cm, same as the body. The foot is ~6.7 cm wide against a ~6.2 cm leg, so a short stub blends them; the ankle join is a smooth blend, not an anatomical ankle.
* **KI5**: 1 B-cun (2.1 cm) below KI3 on the medial view. On the body front/back views the projected KI5 is still a border projection.
* **Foot**: skin = Gray fig. 834's stylised outline (a teaching diagram, not a photograph or a scan) with redrawn toes (capsule shapes; tips and widths are my own proportions, not traced). The dorsal outline is the plantar silhouette mirrored; there is no separate dorsal soft-tissue volume. Bones are schematic vectors positioned from Gray's plates (the plate foot is splayed, so toe bones are shifted ~0.8 cm and follow the skin toe axes); they are not traced bone outlines. Side profiles are hand-drawn from Gray plates 290/291 bone positions plus assumed heights. The malleoli are drawn as shaded domes with an arc, not as a true silhouette bulge, and their position/depth is estimated, so SP5, LR4, ST41, GB40, BL60-BL63, KI3-KI6 inherit that. Toe nail sizes are typical values.
* **Medial/lateral views are new but approximate** (see above). On the body front/back views the projected border points (SP2-4, KI2-6, BL60-66) are still crowded and in projected, not anatomical, depth order; the labels overlap in the feet region of `feet_on_body_front`. Their `basis` says so.
* **Back of head**: points use a simple spherical-scalp model on the silhouette (no skull landmarks); the figure has no ears or hairline on the back view. BL8 is on top of the head and only projected. GB12 is a rough placement. Other scalp and ear points stay on the face view.
* **Side view hand** is a hand-built generic profile (palm + curled fingers + thumb): clean but simplified, with no nails or creases; the shoulder-arm wedge it joins is AcuAtlas's thick arm.
* **Body silhouette**: AcuAtlas's traced outline was smoothed in v1; this pass only re-cut the legs at the ankle (front/back) and replaced the side hand. Its proportions (slender limbs, no body fat) were checked by eye, not measured.
* **Bone layer**: hand bones are Gray's tracing; foot bones are schematic vector drawings (Gray plates 268/269/290/291 as positional reference). Both are illustrative, not a medical image.
* Lines connect points in WHO numbering order per view; **no internal/branch courses** are drawn, and Bladder/Stomach are split into runs. Where the next point is on another view the line stops. LI6-LI15 are mirrored onto the back view as a convenience placement.
* Extra points: EX-UE9/10/11 (hand) and EX-LE8-12 (foot) only; locations from secondary sources, paraphrased. No head/neck/trunk extras, no scalp lines.
* All seven low-overlap WHO wording flags (BL2, BL5, BL7, BL61, KI6, GB13, CV24) were read by hand and mean the same as WHO; the **WHO prose is not shipped**, and this is a wording check on one sentence per point, not a clinical review.
* No 3D: no reliable open-licensed human mesh with meridian data was sourced.
* Palmistry is a traditional layer, not science. Not clinical: the page carries "reference only - placements approximate - not for clinical use".
