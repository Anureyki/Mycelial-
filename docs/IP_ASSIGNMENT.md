## 1. Pre-Existing Work

Assignor is the sole author and owner of a software system known as
**Mycelial OS** ("the System"), including all source code, agent
architectures, inference interfaces, training pipelines, evaluation
harnesses, corpora, documentation, and the federated learning pipeline
incorporating differential privacy, created prior to any employment
relationship and maintained in repositories under Assignor's control.

## 2. Assignment

Assignor hereby assigns to Assignee all right, title, and interest in and
to the System, including all copyrights, trade secrets, and associated
intellectual property rights, worldwide, in perpetuity. Any weights,
parameters, or model artifacts produced through the federated pipeline are
outputs of the System and are included in this Assignment.

## 3. No Work-for-Hire

Nothing in any future employment agreement shall transfer ownership of the
System or its outputs to any employer, unless expressly agreed in a
separate written instrument signed by Assignee.

## 4. Rider

Any employer shall be provided a short rider referencing this Agreement,
stating that the System listed herein is excluded from that employer's IP
assignment clause.
